09 Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Still not sure? Ask Nicholas directly. He usually replies within the hour during shop hours.

Ask on WhatsApp
How much does a CCTV installation cost in Jamaica?

It depends on how many cameras you need and how complex the job is. A basic 4-camera home system starts at around $85,000 JMD installed. We do a free on-site assessment and give you a clear quote with no hidden fees. Commercial systems with video walls are priced on scope.

Do you offer same-day repairs?

Many repairs are done the same day, especially screen replacements and software problems. Bigger hardware repairs usually take 24–72 hours. Call before 2 PM for a same-day diagnostic and quote.

What areas in Jamaica do you serve?

The shop is in Portmore, but we serve the whole island. Walk-in repairs happen at the shop. For CCTV, security and networking installs we travel island-wide, including Kingston, Spanish Town, Ocho Rios, May Pen and beyond.

What's included in the 30-day guarantee?

Every repair gets 30 days of free follow-up. If the same problem comes back or something related goes wrong, bring it in and we'll fix it at no charge. You don't need to find a receipt, because we keep records of every job.

Can I see my CCTV cameras from my phone?

Yes. Every system we install comes with full remote access set up. You can watch live, play back recordings and get motion alerts from anywhere in the world, and we train you on the app before we leave.

Do you sell parts, or only do repairs?

Both. We stock chargers, cases, keyboards, mice and portable power stations, and we sell new and refurbished laptops, tablets and smart TVs. Walk in to browse, or call to check what's in stock.
