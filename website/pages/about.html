Chapter 01

Chapter 01

A knack for fixing things

More than twenty years ago, Nicholas didn't set out to build a tech company. He just had a gift for making broken things work again. Friends brought him computers that had crashed or caught a virus, and he brought them back.

Chapter 02

Word got around

Soon friends of friends were showing up at his door. Then complete strangers. What started as favours became a trusted local name, built entirely on word of mouth and work that held up.

Chapter 03

Beyond the laptop

Today it's much more than laptops and desktops. Nicholas and his team install complex CCTV systems, set up smart home security, automate gates and recover critical data for families and businesses across Jamaica.

Chapter 04

Still in Greater Portmore

The business has grown, but the address and the attitude haven't changed. It's still based right here in Greater Portmore, and when you call, Nicholas still picks up.
