03 CCTV questions

Before you decide.

How much does a CCTV installation cost?

A basic 4-camera home system starts at around $85,000 JMD installed. Larger and commercial systems depend on the number of cameras, cable runs and whether you need a video wall. We quote after a free on-site assessment, with no hidden fees.

Can I watch my cameras from my phone?

Yes, on every system we install. You get live view, playback and motion alerts from anywhere, and we set up the app and walk you through it before we leave.

What happens if the internet goes down?

Recording continues. Footage is stored locally on the NVR (30+ days), so your cameras keep recording even without internet. Remote viewing resumes as soon as the connection is back.

Which areas do you install in?

We're based in Portmore and install across Saint Catherine and Kingston, and island-wide for larger projects, including Spanish Town, Ocho Rios and May Pen.
