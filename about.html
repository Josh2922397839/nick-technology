ABOUT US

The Story of Nick Technology

From fixing friends' computers to serving all of Jamaica.

Hi, I'm Nicholas Anderson.

I didn't start out planning to build a large tech company. More than 20 years ago, I just had a knack for making broken things work again. I started out fixing computers because friends kept bringing me theirs when they crashed or caught a virus. Soon, friends of friends started showing up at my door. Then strangers.

Over two decades later, I'm still doing exactly what I love. Only now, instead of just laptops and desktop PCs, my team and I install complex CCTV setups, set up smart home security networks, automate gates, and recover critical lost data for families and businesses across Jamaica. We've grown, but we're still based right here in Greater Portmore.

“I know what it's like to get overcharged. I promise I'll work with you so you can get quality service at an affordable cost you actually deserve.”

OUR PROMISE

Core Values We Never Compromise On

Honesty

We tell you exactly what's wrong. If it's cheaper for you to buy a new device than for us to fix it, we'll tell you that straight to your face.

Quality

We do things right the first time. We don't cut corners on wiring, we don't use knock-off parts, and we test everything thoroughly before handing it back.

Fair Pricing

No hidden fees. Free diagnostics always. We give you a firm quote before any work begins, so you're never caught off guard.

Personal Service

You're not a ticket number in a queue. We remember our customers, we know their systems, and we care about getting them back online.

Our Mission

To provide honest, reliable, and affordable technology and security solutions to homes and businesses across Jamaica, ensuring no one is left disconnected, overcharged, or unprotected.

Our 5-Year Vision

Over the next 5 years, we aim to expand Nick Technology into the most trusted independent tech and smart security provider in Jamaica. We envision opening multiple service centers, launching a mobile fleet for rapid on-site repairs, and empowering our local community through youth tech apprenticeships.

Why I Still Answer My Own Phone

In a world of automated menus and offshore call centers, when you dial our number, you get me—Nicholas. When you have an emergency with your business security system or a dead laptop with your life's work on it, you don't want to talk to a robot. You want an expert who can solve the problem. That's why I've kept it personal for 20 years.
